# Tokyo Travel Guide

Tokyo is a cluster of cities. Plan by neighborhood rather than by a single downtown. Asakusa and Ueno cover old Tokyo and parks. Shibuya and Shinjuku are nightlife and transit hubs. Ginza and Nihonbashi are calmer and more expensive. Tsukiji Outer Market is for morning food, not the inner tuna auction.

## Local tips

Get a Suica or Pasmo card. Trains stop around midnight. Convenience stores are reliable for breakfast. Many restaurants have ticket machines. Remove shoes when asked. Cash is still useful at small shops even though cards are widely accepted.

## Hidden gems

Yanaka Ginza is a low-rise shopping street with a slower pace. TeamLab Planets is popular but better booked ahead, especially on rainy days. Shimokitazawa is good for vintage shopping. The East Gardens of the Imperial Palace are free and quieter than Shinjuku Gyoen.

## Weather-friendly ideas

Summer is hot and humid; schedule indoor museums, department-store basements, and evening river walks. Rain is common. Cover the Ghibli Museum, teamLab, or a department store food hall when storms roll in. Cherry blossom week is beautiful and crowded; book lodging early.

## FAQs

Do I need a JR Pass? Only if you leave Tokyo for multiple long-distance trips. Inside the city, local metro passes are enough.

Is English usable? Station signage is excellent. Restaurant English varies. Translation apps handle menus well.
